## About

I am a smart contract security researcher focused on identifying high-impact vulnerabilities in DeFi protocols. I have audited a wide range of systems, including perpetual exchanges, stablecoins, tokenized vaults, staking systems, yield protocols, and cross-chain infrastructure.

I have contributed to numerous public audit competitions across Code4rena, Sherlock, Immunefi, and Cantina, earning five podium finishes. This experience has exposed me to different protocol architectures and codebases ranging from approximately 1,000 to more than 10,000 lines of Solidity.

My research focuses on protocol logic, accounting, access control, oracles, and economic vulnerabilities. I combine deep manual analysis with Foundry-based testing, stateless and stateful fuzzing, invariant reasoning, and an attacker-focused review of protocol assumptions.

## Selected Findings

- **Flare FAsset - Medium:** Agent underlying balance can be inflated in a way that cannot be proven or challenged as illegal. [Read the finding](https://code4rena.com/audits/2025-08-flare-fasset/submissions/F-299).
- **Flex Perpetuals - Medium:** Most FTC rewards can be captured by a single entity instead of being distributed among genuine traders. [Read the finding](https://code4rena.com/audits/2024-12-flex-perpetuals/submissions/F-39).
- **Plaza Finance - High:** The pool can be manipulated for financial gain by fluctuating the token supply. [Read the finding](https://github.com/sherlock-audit/2024-12-plaza-finance-judging/issues/935).
- **Flare FAssets - High:** Incorrect implementation of `payout` can cause the `AgentVault` to lose its fee share. [Read the finding](https://reports.immunefi.com/flare-fassets-or-mainnet-audit-comp/46282-sc-high-wrong-implementation-of-payout-would-lead-to-loss-of-fee-share-of-agentvault).
- **Plume Network - High:** Users can claim rewards for periods when their validator was inactive because of incorrect checkpoint accrual. [Read the finding](https://reports.immunefi.com/plume-or-attackathon/51728-sc-high-users-can-claim-rewards-for-inactive-validator-periods-due-to-incorrect-checkpoint-acc).
